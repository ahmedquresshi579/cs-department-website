Welcome to CS Department

Established in 1995, we are committed to excellence in computing education and cutting-edge research.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide rigorous, industry-relevant computer science education that prepares graduates to lead technological innovation globally.

🔬 Research Focus

Our faculty conduct research in AI, cybersecurity, distributed systems, human-computer interaction, and software engineering.

🏆 Achievements

Ranked among the top CS departments in the region with 95% graduate employment rate and partnerships with 50+ tech companies.

📚 Programs Offered

We offer BS, MS, and PhD programs in Computer Science along with short certificate courses in emerging technologies.

👩‍💻 Student Life

Active coding clubs, hackathons, tech talks, and industry visits make CS student life dynamic and career-oriented.

🤝 Industry Links

Strong ties with Google, Microsoft, and local tech firms provide students with internship and placement opportunities.
