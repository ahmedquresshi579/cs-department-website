Degree Programs

We offer programs at undergraduate and graduate levels.

🎓 BS Computer Science

4-year undergraduate program. 130 credit hours. Covers core CS fundamentals, electives, and a final year project.

🎓 MS Computer Science

2-year graduate program. 30 credit hours. Course-based or thesis track available. Specializations in AI, Security, and Systems.

🎓 PhD Computer Science

3–5 year research doctorate. Requires MS degree. Fully funded positions available for qualified candidates.

Application Steps

Follow these steps to complete your application.

Create an Account

Register on the university admissions portal using your CNIC and a valid email address.

Fill the Application Form

Complete all sections of the online form including personal, academic, and program details.

Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of your transcripts, CNIC, domicile, and passport-size photograph.

Pay the Application Fee

Pay Rs. 2,000 via the university online payment system or designated bank branch.

Appear for Entry Test

Sit for the university entrance test or submit valid NTS/GAT scores as applicable.

Merit List & Enrollment

Check the merit list on the portal. If selected, complete enrollment within the deadline.
